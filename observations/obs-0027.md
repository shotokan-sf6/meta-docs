title: Fireball Drive Rush pays less than a raw rush, and the follow-up decides it
evidence: [player:Zastorial, player:Broski]
---
Across the database's fireball characters (`tools/full_review_fireball_drive_rush.py`, ranked sets), a fireball followed by a Drive Rush netted +156 damage per use over the next 30 frames (333 uses, 1.33 Drive bars each). A raw Drive Rush netted +224 for about 1 bar (1,402 uses). 73% of fireball rushes were blocked. Round win rate was 60.5% when a player's fireball rushes netted positive in the round, 34.6% when they netted negative, and 43% when they used none; within the same player the gap was about 6 points.

What happens after the first blocked button decides the value. In Zastorial's A.K.I. sets, a second mid strike lost 470 on average (19 uses), while a low (+894) or a throw (+740, landing 57%) won. Broski spends 1.1 bars per fireball rush to Zastorial's 1.9: Zastorial uses more OD Nightshade Pulse.
