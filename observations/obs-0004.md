title: Winners are more patient on defense
evidence: [page:/analysis/traits]

Winners held block after blocking a string 21% of the time to the loser's 18% (higher in 62% of sets) and held block on wake-up 21% vs 18% (63%). They also took less of their damage on wake-up: 10% of damage taken came in oki, against 14% for losers (losers higher in 67% of sets). Their Initiative spectrum is lower (2.7 vs 3.1) and their defensive Temperament more mindful (3.2 vs 2.7).

E.X. Dojo's Defense and Offense 101: blocking is the default, especially when you're ahead on life and Drive; spend actions only when you have a reason. The data suggests winners don't attack less overall (decision presses per round are even, 12.3 vs 12.7) but they wait more at the moments that get punished.
