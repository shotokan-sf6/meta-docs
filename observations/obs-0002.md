Winners landed 2.0 throws a game to the loser's 1.3 and threw more in 74% of sets that weren't ties, one of the steadiest signals on the page. The clearest single example is AZ_Young02 vs PepsiHeart (FT5): 15 throws in games 1-4 (3-1), none in games 5-7 (0-3), then 5 in games 8-9 (2-0).

Coach DoctaAfrikan's point from his sessions with Sean: the throw is the one option that always gives a definite answer, so put it in your default offense, and against low-risk players keep throwing until they tech. A tech also tells you something, which is what Improving Your Shimmies (E.X. Dojo) builds on.
