The 22 players flagged notable, against their own (non-notable) opponents in the same sets: 139 sets, 93 won, with a median MR of 2039 against 1918, so part of every gap is rank. Only six notable players have five or more such sets, so these are leans.

Two differences held for all six: more counter hits (2.33 a round to 1.67) and more time cornering than cornered (22% to 14% of live time). Throws landed held for five of six (0.85 to 0.64). For four of six: more damage per decision press (657 to 503) with about the same number of presses, more combo Drive Rush cancels and fewer pressure cancels into a block, more super spent, fewer jumps, more anti-airs, and holding block after blocking a little more often.

What didn't separate them: close punishes landed (55% to 58%), throw techs, raw Drive Rush and burnouts. And notable players ended a larger share of their lost rounds with 3 bars of super still unused (40% to 34%): spending super separates winners from losers in general, but not these players from theirs.
