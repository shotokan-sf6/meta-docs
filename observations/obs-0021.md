title: Pressing after blocking doesn't separate winners

Across the 145 decided sets, winners and losers press after blocking a string at the same rate. Winners pressed on the first free frame after blockstun 18.3% of the time and losers 18.9% (the winner was higher in 47% of sets). Any attack as the first action after blocking was 34.9% vs 35.9%, and attacking on wake-up 27.6% vs 28.4% (the winner was higher in 45%).

So the count of early presses isn't a leak by itself. It becomes one when those presses keep getting hit, which is what the "Pressed after turn ended" lens measures. .shog's 25 Nov set against 310Sho shows the difference: he pressed on the first free frame 8 times in 30, inside his usual range, but two of those presses took 7,050 damage (27% of what he took), the most of any lens in the set.

Reviews should report the damage those presses cost, and what the opponent did to catch them (a frame trap, a delayed button, a reversal), rather than the rate. This builds on obs-0010: winners don't hold block more, they get hit less when they don't.
