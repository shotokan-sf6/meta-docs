Across 130 reviewed sets (winner vs loser of the same set, MR-weighted), winners made 1.9 combo Drive Rush cancels a game to the loser's 1.0, and had the higher count in 74% of sets. Their Resource use spectrum was 3.9 to 2.4 (higher in 87% of sets), the widest gap on the page: winners spend more Drive, but they spend it after a hit.

Pressure Drive Rush cancels on block still don't separate them (1.02 vs 1.03 a game; the winner was higher in 46% of sets). The 3 bars buy something when they extend a confirmed hit, and usually nothing when they extend a blocked string.

E.X. Dojo's hit-confirm seminar makes the same point: confirm before spending Drive, and confirm off the Drive Rush normal, not off 2MK. Juzzy calls it cashing out: spend meter when it turns an opening into damage or a kill.

Updated 2026-11-23: 122 sets (was 69). Updated 2026-11-24: 130 sets (was 122). The finding held.
