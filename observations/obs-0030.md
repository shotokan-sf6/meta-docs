A correction to the A.K.I. notes behind obs-0027. A.K.I. has only two fireballs: light Nightshade Pulse (214LP, 52 frames to come out) and OD (214PP, 44 frames). Her medium and heavy inputs (Orchid Spring, Toxic Wreath) aren't fireballs, so the usual light-versus-heavy fireball rules don't apply to her. Her Pulse is slower than most fireballs, so it is easy to perfect parry or jump on reaction, and its job is to start offense (Drive Rush or walk behind it), not to zone.

The 2026-11-26 Zastorial sets show both sides of that: three opponents jumped a Pulse on reaction (2,480 to 2,800 each) and a Terry counter-hit two thrown at 2MK range, while fireball Drive Rush itself paid +353 a use. Reviews now judge A.K.I.'s fireballs by what she does behind them, and A.K.I. reviews that suggest Sinister Slide must name the ending (Venomous Fang, Heel Strike, Entrapment or the stance cancel).
