title: Adaptability asks whether the opponent's best weapon kept working
supersedes: obs-0008

Adaptability (Inelastic to Elastic) has a new measure. The old one counted how often a player kept using moves that had been punished, only while losing (obs-0008). It sat at 3 in 38% of sets, and set winners scored lower on it than losers, so it mostly caught losing players changing things because they were losing.

The new measure looks at the other side: the move that did the most damage to the player in the first half of the set's rounds (at least 2,500), and how much damage per round it still did in the second half. If it stopped working, the player adjusted (elastic); if it kept working, they didn't. Now set winners score more elastic than losers in 53% of sets and less in 29%, and only 20% of scores sit at 3. It needs four rounds, so a 2-0 sweep of short rounds may have no score. Reviewer overrides from Full reviews still apply.
