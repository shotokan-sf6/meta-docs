Since 2026-11-21, the Adaptability (elastic to inelastic) spectrum only counts a reused move against a player while they were losing that game or the move kept getting punished. Keeping a winning plan scores 3/5. With that rule, winners and losers score about the same (3.0 vs 3.2).

That is the intended result: not changing a winning plan isn't inelastic. The spectrum is now for spotting players who keep doing what is losing, not for ranking everyone. One gap remains: it counts reuse, not whether the move kept costing them. AZ_Young02 scored 1/5 while his blocked sweep stopped being punished after game 4, so the reviewer's call overrode it.

Updated 2026-11-23: 122 sets (was 69). The finding held.

Spectra re-scored 2026-11-24 against everyone except .shog (each other player weighted equally); the spectrum figures here are from that scale.
