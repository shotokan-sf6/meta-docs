topic: Model change
title: Burnouts no longer separate winners from losers
evidence: [page:/analysis/traits]
---
Since 2026-11-22, a burnout spent on a combo that kills (for example everything into SA3 for the kill) is logged as a lethal burnout and no longer counted. With that rule, winners and losers burn out at the same rate (0.40 vs 0.39 a game; the winner had more in 47% of sets).

The model's view changes from 'burnouts lose games' to 'what the Drive bought decides it'. Combo cancels separate winners (obs-0001) and pressure cancels don't. Burnouts are now a symptom to read in context, not a leak on their own.

Updated 2026-11-23: 122 sets (was 69). Updated 2026-11-24: 130 sets (was 122). The finding held.
