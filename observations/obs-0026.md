title: High-level A.K.I. wins on the throw loop
evidence: [player:Broski, player:Zastorial]
---
Broski (about 2,000 MR, 20 ranked sets, won 15) threw 1.6 times a round; Zastorial (about 1,530 MR, 15 sets, won 4) threw 0.7. Throws were 24.6% of Broski's damage and 8.9% of Zastorial's. Broski threw again straight after a throw 80 times, often three to five in a row; Zastorial did it 13 times. When the opponent started teching, Broski's delayed 5HK / 2LP / 5LP / 2MK counter-hit the tech attempt 33 times for 68,378 (538 a round, to Zastorial's 279).

The neutral numbers point the other way: Zastorial landed more counter-hits (2.1 a round to 1.7) and more damage per decision press (604 to 561). The gap is the knockdown, not the buttons. Broski's losses came from players who frame-trapped or punished his throw range (MysticSmash, Asahi, Rumle) or adapted over repeat sets (Boltstrike won the fourth). When reviewing an A.K.I., count throw-after-throw and shimmy counter-hits.
