Recounted over every graded pre-set prediction, leaving out the ones written after the result was seen: 82 of 111 picked the winner (74%). Picking the higher-MR player wins 93 of the 133 sets with both MRs known (70%). In obs-0016 the two were level at 72%.

The gap is in the confidence labels. Low-confidence predictions are right 63% of the time (41 of 65), about what MR alone gives. Medium is 86% (31 of 36) and High 8 of 8. The method from obs-0016 is unchanged: start from the MR gap, go against it only with direct evidence, and store the prediction blind before the analyzer runs.

On 25 Nov, 8 of 10 blind predictions were right, including both rematches (Alexmoim and RD|Onyx), where the earlier set that day was known. Both misses were Low-confidence calls on near-even MR (hitei, Alexmoim).
