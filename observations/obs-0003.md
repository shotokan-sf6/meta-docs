Counter hits: 5.9 a game for winners, 4.7 for losers (winner higher in 69% of sets). Punish counters: 0.8 vs 0.4 (69%). Damage dealt separates, as you'd expect, but these two say where it comes from: beating the opponent's button with yours, and punishing what they leave unsafe.

This is the Economy of Buttons idea in .shog's own coaching rules: let the opponent press, then make each of their presses cost them.

Updated 2026-11-23: 122 sets (was 69). Updated 2026-11-24: 130 sets (was 122). The finding held.
