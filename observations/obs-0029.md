title: Corner time is now measured from every capture
evidence: [player:.shog, player:ll Moke ll, player:Broski, player:Zastorial]
---
Since 2026-11-27 the analyzer measures how long each player spends cornered in every round (`tools/corner_time.py`). A player counts as cornered on a frame when they stand within 1.5 of the wall (the wall is at |posX| 7.65) and the opponent is on the inner side of them; only live play counts (no round intro, KO or outro). Each round stores the seconds cornered, how many times the player got cornered and the longest stretch, and every set in the database (283 sets, 717 games) was backfilled from its capture.

The capture can't tell a player who is pinned from a zoner who parked in the corner on purpose: both count as cornered. First numbers, as a share of live time cornered and cornering the opponent: .shog 12% and 22%, ll Moke ll 15% and 22%, Broski 17% and 19%, Zastorial 18% and 19%.
