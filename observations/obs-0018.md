Of the 79 pre-set predictions that picked the right winner, 58 also got the margin and 21 undersold it. None overrated the winner's margin. In .shog's wins it's 9 of 17, including all three training sets on 24 Nov (obi1 4-1 predicted, 5-0 played; Baol 4-3, 5-2; Solid 4-3, 5-2).

The predictions hedge toward a close score even when the winner pick is right. When a prediction backs the favourite (the higher MR, with earlier wins in the matchup), its score should lean a game wider; the winner pick still starts from MR (obs-0016).
