title: Jumping out of Broski's throw loop didn't pay
evidence: [player:Broski, player:Zastorial]
---
Opponents jumped on 5% of their wake-ups against Broski's A.K.I. (19 of 390), and on 3% after one of his throws (4 of 145). Against Zastorial they jumped on 14% of wake-ups on 2026-11-26 (30 of 209), and on 20% after his throws (7 of 35). When they did jump against Broski he hit them 15 times out of 17 (25,788 damage to 720), mostly with 5HK.

It isn't that Broski strikes more on oki: throws are 48% of his oki hits and 14% of Zastorial's. A wake-up jump held from the knockdown always beats a throw, but nobody can jump on reaction to one, so jumping out of a throw loop is a read. The likely reason opponents stop making that read against Broski is that it didn't pay: when they jumped, he hit them almost every time. Three of the four throw attempts jumped against Zastorial on 2026-11-26 were throws in neutral, where the walk-in itself gives the opponent time to decide. The two times a jump beat Broski's throw and the round went on, his next knockdown got one meaty strike (2LP, 5HK) and then three throws in a row.
