title: A meaty throw loop doesn't get jumped
evidence: [player:Broski, player:Zastorial]
---
Opponents jumped on 5% of their wake-ups against Broski's A.K.I. (19 of 390), and on 3% after one of his throws (4 of 145). Against Zastorial they jumped on 14% of wake-ups on 2026-11-26 (30 of 209), and on 20% after his throws (7 of 35). When they did jump against Broski he hit them 15 times out of 17 (25,788 damage to 720), mostly with 5HK.

It isn't that Broski strikes more on oki: throws are 48% of his oki hits and 14% of Zastorial's. The likely reason is timing. A throw on the wake-up frame catches the first frames of a jump, so a meaty throw loop leaves the jump nowhere to go. A walk-up or late throw whiffs under it: three of the four throw attempts jumped against Zastorial on 2026-11-26 were throws in neutral. The two times a jump beat Broski's throw and the round went on, his next knockdown got one meaty strike (2LP, 5HK) and then three throws in a row.
