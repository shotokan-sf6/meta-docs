Since 2026-11-27 every player read has a Space spectrum, Gives ground to Takes ground: the share of live time a player spends cornering the opponent minus the share spent cornered (from the corner time in obs-0029). It is calibrated like the other spectra, against every player except the configured one.

The set winner scored higher on Space in 76% of the 268 finished sets that had both scores, more often than on any other measure the model keeps (Resource use was next at 71%). Part of that is the result rather than the cause: a player who is winning exchanges pushes the opponent back. It still says something useful: across the six notable players with enough sets, every one spent more time cornering than cornered. A zoner who backs into the corner on purpose reads as giving ground; the capture can't tell parking from being pinned.
