Since 2026-11-26 the analyzer names these characters' specials from the SuperCombo move lists, matched to the internal codes by the inputs in the captures. Three of A.K.I.'s old labels were wrong. What reviews called "Snake Step" is Sinister Slide (2PP), and its grab is Entrapment. "Cruel Fate" is Snake Step (236K). The real Cruel Fate (214K) was unnamed. Jamie's SPA3 is Arrow Kick, SPA5 is Tenshin and SPA_4 is Luminous Dive Kick. Alex's command grab is Power Bomb and his stance moves are Prowler Stance. Elena's 623K is Scratch Wheel, not Spinning Scythe.

Stored hits, timings and usage were relabelled in place, and the 35 A.K.I. reviews were re-ingested with corrected text. Read "Snake Step" in A.K.I. reviews written before this date as Sinister Slide.
