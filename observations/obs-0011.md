Since 2026-11-23, a hit's damage is the permanent life the victim lost, read from the recoverable-health cap, not the drop in the red bar. A hit that lands while the victim has white (recoverable) health cashes that white health in, and it now counts toward the hit. Every reviewed set with a capture was re-scored: 285 games, 1,131 hits changed, 456,450 more damage in total. The 11 video-only games keep the old count.

Before the change, lost rounds often didn't add up to a full life bar (10 of 23 rounds in one C.Viper set); now 2 do, and the small gap left is permanent chip, which the analyzer still doesn't count.

What this changes on the site: damage taken by situation and lens can shift toward whatever follows blocked pressure and Drive Reversals, since that is when white health is on the bar. Counts (counter hits, throws, Drive Rushes) didn't change.
