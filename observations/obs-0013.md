title: Winners start more with raw Drive Rush
evidence: [page:/analysis/traits]

Winners made 3.4 raw Drive Rushes a game (from neutral, 1 bar) to the loser's 2.6, and made more in 62% of sets. It's a weaker signal than throws or combo cancels, but it points the same way as obs-0001: Drive spent cheaply to start offense or after a hit pays; 3 bars spent extending a blocked string doesn't.

A raw Drive Rush costs one bar plus a hit if it's checked, and the Drive Rush button gets +4, so it's a fairly low-risk way to start pressure. It only becomes a leak when the opponent keeps checking it and the player doesn't adjust; reviews should look for that rather than counting raw rushes as aggression on their own.

Updated 2026-11-24: 130 sets (was 122). The finding held.
