Across 977 lost rounds in the 145 decided sets, the player who lost the round ended it with 2.2 bars of super on average, against 1.6 for the round's winner. 59% of lost rounds ended with at least 2 bars unspent and 35% with a full 3. Drive tells the same story: 2.7 bars left at the end of a lost round, 2.0 for the winner.

Round winners spent 1.1 bars of super a round to the loser's 0.3. Part of that gap is likely the winner spending super to close the round out, so not every unspent bar was a missed chance. But a full gauge at the KO is damage that was never used, and it happens in a third of lost rounds.

.shog is no exception: 78 of his 142 lost rounds ended with 2 or more bars and 41 with 3. Juzzy calls the fix cashing out: when a hit can carry into super, spend it, especially when the round is close and the next interaction might be the last.

This extends obs-0015 (super is under-used: 1.9 bars left at each round's end) from all rounds to lost ones.
