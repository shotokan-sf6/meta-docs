title: Cash-in: where the Drive goes, not how much

A new spectrum, Cash-in (Speculative to Cashes in), measures the share of a player's Drive and super spending that went into combos that landed. Resource use already says how much a player spends, and set winners spend more (the traits page); Cash-in separates converting from guessing. The set winner scored higher on Cash-in in 68% of sets and lower in 17%. It overlaps only partly with Resource use (correlation 0.29), so the two read together: spendthrift and cashing in is converting hits into damage, spendthrift and speculative is Drive spent on rushes, parries and OD moves that didn't connect.

The notable-player comparison points the same way: against their own opponents they did more combo Drive Rush cancels (0.63 a round to 0.42) and fewer pressure cancels into a blocked string (0.30 to 0.49).
