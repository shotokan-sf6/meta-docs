With 130 sets, most of the 'patient defense' gap from obs-0004 is gone. Winners held block after blocking a string 23% of the time to the loser's 21% (higher in 55% of sets), and held block on wake-up at the same rate (20% vs 21%, 51%). Their Initiative spectrum is even (3.1 vs 3.2) and defensive Temperament nearly so (3.2 vs 3.2).

What still separates them is the result on wake-up, not the habit: 11% of the winner's damage taken came in oki, against 14% for the loser (the loser took the bigger share in 67% of sets). Winners don't block more on wake-up, they get hit less there, which points at the choice of option (and the attacker's oki) more than at how often they hold block.

So E.X. Dojo's Defense and Offense 101 still applies, but the data no longer says 'block more'. Reviews should look at which wake-up options got hit (pressed into a meaty, teched into a shimmy, jumped into an anti-air) rather than counting held blocks.

Updated 2026-11-24: 130 sets (was 122). The finding held.
