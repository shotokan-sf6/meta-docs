topic: Model change
title: DRD in a victim move means they were already hit
evidence: [player:Zastorial]
---
In hit rows, a victim move such as `5060_DRD_STD_L_START` or `5090_DRD_CRH_H_START` means the victim was already in hit reaction (standing or crouching, light to heavy) when the new hit landed: the analyzer split a combo or caught a reset. It does not mean the victim was in Drive Rush (that shows as `Drive Rush`) or crouch-blocking (`GRD_` states). Three 2026-11-26 Zastorial reviews had read these rows as 2MK checking a Drive Rush or 3MP opening a crouch-blocker; they were corrected and re-ingested.

Likewise a move the victim was "in" when grabbed (a `HOLD` state, sometimes only a number such as `_966`) is the being-grabbed animation: the decision to read is the move just before it.
