On 23 Nov .shog played three training sets right after comparing his decision presses with Coosco and Hollywood RobTV. The offense lessons stuck at once: more raw Drive Rush (3.5 to 4.7 a game against his usual 1.3), more throws on oki, and 5MK / 5HK instead of 2MP at mid range.

The defense lessons held only against weaker or familiar opponents. Against Exous (MR well below his) he pressed on the first free frame after blocking 13% of the time (usual 17%) and his presses after blocking got him hit 5% of the time. Against XcelotL, the closest in MR (1667), it went to 29%, and 2MP came back: 12 counter hits for 23,633, 60% of his mid-range 2MPs getting him hit.

Habits on offense change in a session; defensive habits come back under pressure and need reps against players at or above your level before they hold.
