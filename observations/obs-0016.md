id: obs-0016
date: 2026-11-23
topic: Model change
title: Predictions start from MR and are stored blind
evidence: [player:.shog, player:DaFeetLee]
---
Pre-set predictions picked the winner in 72% of 110 sets (79), and the higher-MR player won 72% of the 109 sets with both MRs known. The reads didn't beat MR. The DaFeetLee set showed why: the prediction went against a +24 MR edge because the matchup (C.Viper) was new to .shog, and .shog won 2-0.

Since 2026-11-23, predictions start from the MR gap and go against it only with direct evidence. They are also stored blind: the pre-set facts come from the first game's header only (later games' MR gives earlier results away), history comes only from earlier sets, and the prediction is stored in the database before the analyzer runs, so it can't be changed after the result is known.

Updated 2026-11-24: recounted over every pre-set prediction (was 92 sets); the predictions still match MR, they don't beat it. The three blind predictions on 24 Nov all picked the right winner (see obs-0018).
