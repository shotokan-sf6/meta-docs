In 173 games with per-game press counts, the winner made 51% of the game's decision presses on average (between 43% and 59% in 80% of games) and pressed more in only 54% of games. Across 125 sets with press counts, decision presses per round are even too (12.7 vs 12.9).

What separates them is damage per decision press: the winner averaged 778 to the loser's 463 and was higher in 89% of games, the strongest single signal in the data. It holds for individual players: .shog 645 vs his opponents' 474, Cheez 695 vs 490, jueks 601 vs 392.

This is the Economy of Buttons in .shog's coaching rules: the share of buttons is close to even for everyone; the game is won by whose presses land as counter hits and punishes and whose get caught.
