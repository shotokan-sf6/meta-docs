title: Against Arjun, blocking isn't free

Arjun's blocked pressure and stance counter leave white health on the opponent's bar, and his next hit cashes all of it in. In the six reviewed sets with an Arjun, 24% to 36% of his damage came from white health cashed in (59,241 of 209,307 in total). No other character is close: Cammy, with 91 sets, cashed in 6,936.

That changes the defense equation for this matchup. Holding block against long Arjun strings is cheaper than getting hit, but not free the way it is against most characters: the blocked damage is waiting for the next hit. Cheez's only lost set in ten was against Arjun, with 9,286 white health cashed in. Getting out of his pressure (a parry, a jump-out or Drive Reversal at the right gap, or just ending his turn) is worth more here than usual, and the white health regenerates if he can't land the hit.
