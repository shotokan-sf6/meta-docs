evidence: [page:/analysis/traits]

Across 367 captured games (median MR about 1740): a game is 2.4 rounds (42% go to a third round) and about 2 minutes 13 seconds; a round is about 55 seconds and about 11 openings (9.6 damaging interactions plus 1.3 throws), so the winner needs about 7 or 8.

Nearly half of the damaging openings are counter hits (10.6 a game) and 56% of damage comes from neutral. Each player lands about 1.6 throws and teches 0.45, makes about 2 jump-ins (about half get anti-aired) and 3.0 raw Drive Rushes a game.

Each player spends 6.7 Drive bars a round, more than a full gauge, and still ends the round with 2.3: regeneration pays for most of it. Super is under-used: 0.7 bars spent a round, 1.9 left at each round's end.

Updated 2026-11-24: 130 sets (was 122). Games counted: 367 (was 332). Game and round length and the jump-in figures are still from the 23 Nov count.
