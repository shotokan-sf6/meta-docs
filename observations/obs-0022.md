Block punish windows (the attacker still recovering by 5 or more frames when the defender leaves blockstun) have been measured since 24 Nov, in 12 sets so far. Close windows (1.6 or less) were punished 7 times out of 12. Mid-range windows (up to 2.6) were punished once in 37, and far ones never (0 of 6). The windows marked "wiki disagrees" are left out.

A -5 or -6 move at mid range is usually only punishable with a fast long-range button or a Drive Rush, and most defenders held, walked, jumped or pressed a button that didn't reach. In the 310Sho set .shog had 6 mid-range windows (Ken's 5HK and 2MK at -5 and -6) and punished none.

Until there are more sets, reviews should treat a missed close window as a missed punish and a missed mid-range one as a lab question (which button, if any, reaches), not a leak. The sample is small: recount once 30 or more sets have punish data.
