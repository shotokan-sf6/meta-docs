title: Archetypes recalibrated: four reads instead of two

Generated (Quick review) archetype reads used fixed totals per set, such as 12 forward jumps or 8 Drive Rushes, so long sets and jump-heavy characters came out Aggressive: 56% of all reads were Aggressive, 37% Balanced, and Defensive and Fundamentalist together under 8%. Since 2026-11-27 they use per-round rates against the pool of every player except the configured one: pressing after blocking or on wake-up, Drive Rush pressure, and forward jumps relative to that character's average make an aggression score; counter hits and punish counters per round mark the fundamentals. Aggressive is the top 30% on aggression, Defensive the bottom 30%, Fundamentalist the top 30% on counter hits without being aggressive, and Balanced the rest. Hand-written Full-review reads were kept.

Ranked reads now split Aggressive 38%, Balanced 32%, Fundamentalist 18%, Defensive 13%. Fundamentalist players won 75% of their ranked sets and Defensive players 27%. Read the Fundamentalist number with care: landing counter hits is part of the definition and also comes with winning, and many of those reads are one strong Mai player (ll Moke ll).
