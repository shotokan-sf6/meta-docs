On 25 Nov, reversals the opponent had already shown beat .shog's oki and pressure 9 times in two ranked sets for 15,264. hitei's OD Tensenrin went through his pressure five times in one set (8,504). Alexmoim's OD Tiger Uppercut was blocked and punished twice in the morning set and he stopped using it. In the rematch two hours later he used it again on his wake-up, and it beat .shog's 2HP, 5MP and 5LP four times (6,760) before one was blocked and punished. In the FuyuKaki training set, the Raging Demon that won G1 came back as a meaty Raging Demon on his wake-up in G4.

A reversal that was punished isn't gone, it's resting. A new set, a new game or a low-life round with Drive or super brings it back, and the attacker who stopped respecting it pays (structure note #1: oki is a read). In .shog's ranked sets that day, "own offense beaten by a reversal" was 6.7% of his damage taken.

The fix is cheap: on the first oki of a new set, and whenever the defender has 2+ Drive or 3 super, walk up and block (or shimmy back) once before pressing, and punish the reversal if it comes. See obs-0017 (lessons stick until the pressure rises).
